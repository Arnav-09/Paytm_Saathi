Policy Name: Demo Shield Gold Plan
Insurer Name: Demo Health Shield Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)
Policy Type: Indemnity
Policy Number: DHS-2025-004711
Policy Period: 1 Jan 2025 to 31 Dec 2025
Policy Start Date: 01/01/2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Definitions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Hospitalisation means admission in a hospital for at least 24 consecutive hours.
1.2 Pre-existing disease means any condition diagnosed before the policy start date.
1.3 Network hospital means a hospital listed on our cashless network.
1.4 Day care procedure means a treatment that does not need 24 hours of admission.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Sum Insured
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 The Sum Insured under this policy is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) per policy year.
2.2 Restoration: once exhausted, the Sum Insured is restored once in a policy year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Room Rent, Boarding and Nursing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Room rent is limited to Rs. 5,000 per day for a single private room.
3.2 ICU charges are limited to Rs. 10,000 per day.
3.3 If you choose a room costlier than the limit, all doctor fees, surgery charges and nursing charges are reduced in the same proportion.
3.4 Medicines, diagnostics and consumables are not affected by the room limit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Co-payment
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 The policyholder shall bear a co-payment of 10% of every admissible claim.
4.2 Co-payment is applied after the deductible.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Deductible
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 A deductible of Rs. 10,000 applies to each claim before co-payment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Waiting Periods
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Initial waiting period: 30 days from the policy start date, except for accidents.
6.2 Pre-existing diseases: covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.
6.3 Specified diseases (cataract, hernia, knee replacement): 24 months.
6.4 Maternity: 9 months of continuous coverage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Exclusions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Cosmetic or aesthetic surgery, except reconstructive surgery after an accident.
7.2 Dental treatment, unless caused by accident.
7.3 Weight loss programmes, bariatric surgery and obesity treatment.
7.4 Injuries from hazardous sports such as mountaineering or motor racing.
7.5 Infertility and sex-change treatment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Sub-limits
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Cataract surgery is limited to Rs. 40,000 per eye per policy year.
8.2 Maternity expenses are limited to Rs. 50,000 per policy year.
8.3 Diagnostic tests done outside the hospital are limited to Rs. 5,000 per year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Pre and Post Hospitalisation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before admission are covered.
9.2 Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge are covered.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Claim Conditions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10.1 Cashless: inform us 48 hours before a planned admission, or within 24 hours of an emergency admission, and submit the pre-authorisation form.
10.2 Reimbursement: submit the claim form, original bills and discharge summary within 30 days of discharge.
10.3 Claims for accidents must include an FIR or Medico-Legal Certificate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Grievance
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11.1 For complaints, write to the Grievance Officer at grievance@demo-shield.example and escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman if unresolved in 30 days.
