# Insurance claims and grievances

## Making a cashless claim
1. Choose a network hospital from your insurer's list. 2. At the hospital's insurance desk, show your health card and ID and fill the pre-authorisation form. 3. The insurer reviews it and approves, partly approves or asks for more information. 4. At discharge, the final bill is settled directly between hospital and insurer. You pay only non-payable items, co-payment, deductible and any amount above limits.

## Making a reimbursement claim
Pay the hospital, keep every original bill and receipt, and collect the discharge summary, investigation reports, prescriptions and pharmacy bills. Submit the claim form with these documents and your bank details to the insurer or TPA within the time limit in your policy (often 15 to 30 days after discharge). Pre- and post-hospitalisation bills have their own submission windows.

## Documents for accident cases
An FIR or medico-legal certificate is usually needed for accidents, along with the doctor's notes. Keep the first-aid and emergency bills too.

## Why claims are reduced or rejected
Common reasons include a waiting period not completed, a pre-existing disease not disclosed, a treatment that is excluded, missing or unclear documents, delay in intimation or submission, a room costlier than the limit, and charges above sub-limits. Many reductions are avoidable if you check the policy before admission.

## If a claim is rejected or reduced
Ask the insurer for the rejection reason in writing and read the clause it cites. Send a written representation with any missing documents. If you are not satisfied, escalate in this order: the insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer; then the IRDAI Bima Bharosa complaint portal (bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in) or toll-free number; then the Insurance Ombudsman of your area if the insurer did not respond within 30 days or you are unhappy with the reply. Consumer courts are the last option. Complaints to the Ombudsman have to be filed within one year of the insurer's final reply and are free of charge.

## Complaints about banks, wallets and UPI
First complain to the bank or payment provider and note the complaint number. If you get no satisfactory reply within 30 days, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman through the RBI Complaint Management System (cms.rbi.org.in).

## Fraud and cybercrime
If you lose money to a digital fraud, call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Quick reporting improves the chance of freezing the money. Tell your bank in writing. Under RBI customer-protection rules, reporting an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days gives the strongest protection against loss; later reporting may limit it.
