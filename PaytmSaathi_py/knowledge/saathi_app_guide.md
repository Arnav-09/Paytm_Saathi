# How to use Paytm Saathi

## What Saathi is
Paytm Saathi helps you understand insurance, loans and payments in simple language, in English and several Indian languages. It explains and estimates; it does not sell insurance or loans, settle claims or approve anything. Final decisions belong to the insurer, lender or bank.

## Insurance: My policy
Upload your health policy PDF (a scanned copy works too) in Insurance, then My policy. Saathi reads it and shows the sum insured, room rent limit, co-pay, exclusions and waiting periods with the clause and page. The Coverage at a glance section shows what is covered, what is not, and the watch-outs. Enter the policy start date to see when each waiting period ends.

## Insurance: Ask Saathi
Ask questions about your own policy. Answers come only from your policy text and show the page and clause they came from. If the policy does not say, Saathi tells you instead of guessing.

## Insurance: Claim Pre-Flight
Upload a hospital bill or a cost estimate (PDF, scan or photo), correct the line items, and get a step-by-step estimate of what the insurer may pay and what you may pay. It shows non-payable items, room rent cap, proportionate deduction, sub-limits, deductible, co-pay and the remaining sum insured. The room what-if shows how choosing a different room changes your payout. The result is an estimate, not an approval.

## Loans
Use the EMI calculator and the prepayment planner to see interest saved by paying extra. The eligibility check gives an indicative result using illustrative rules and shows what would improve it. Compare offers shows the true cost including fees. These are guides and not offers from any lender.

## Payments: Payment problem
Describe what went wrong. Saathi suggests the steps, shows escalation dates and drafts a complaint letter for the app, the bank or the RBI Ombudsman. You can attach screenshots or the SMS text to the ticket and download an evidence pack to send with your complaint.

## Payments: Scam check
Paste a suspicious message and, if you know it, the sender. Saathi checks the wording, links, phone numbers and sender, and highlights risky parts. It cannot prove a message is safe, so confirm anything important in the official app.

## Privacy
Your account holds only basic details. Saathi hides OTPs, PINs, card numbers, Aadhaar and PAN in chat and in saved evidence. KYC documents are not collected at sign-up.
